Safety Checklist

Use these steps before meeting, paying, or completing a sale.

Before You Meet

  • Ask for clear photos, vehicle details, title status, and VIN information where appropriate.
  • Be cautious of rushed decisions, unusually low prices, or requests for immediate deposits.
  • Bring a friend and tell someone where you are going.
  • Arrange to meet in a public, well-lit location during daylight hours.

Before You Pay

  • Arrange an independent inspection by a qualified mechanic or RV specialist.
  • Verify the seller’s identity and confirm ownership and lien information.
  • Verify cashier’s checks directly with the issuing bank before accepting them.
  • Avoid gift cards, cryptocurrency pressure, unknown wire recipients, and overpayment/refund schemes.

Test Drives & Walkthroughs

  • Confirm the driver has a valid license and appropriate insurance.
  • Agree on a route, time limit, and who will be present.
  • For RVs, review appliances, slides, generator systems, maintenance records, and visible condition.
  • Document condition and questions with photos and written notes.

Documents & Closing

  • Confirm the VIN matches the vehicle and relevant paperwork.
  • Review title status, registration, liens, and any required state paperwork.
  • Use a bank, credit union, or reputable escrow provider when appropriate.
  • Do not transfer funds until the agreed documentation and payment steps are complete.
Important: No online marketplace can eliminate every risk. Take your time, verify independently, and stop the transaction if information does not match or someone pressures you.

Common Scam Warning Signs

Pause and investigate if you notice any of the following.

Payment Red Flags

  • Requests for gift cards, cryptocurrency, or payment to an unrelated third party.
  • Overpayment followed by a request to refund the difference.
  • Pressure to pay before an inspection, viewing, or document review.
  • Shipping or escrow stories that require immediate payment to an unknown party.

Identity & Listing Red Flags

  • Refusal to provide reasonable vehicle details or additional photos.
  • VIN, title, location, or seller information that does not match.
  • Requests to move communication off-platform immediately.
  • Urgency, secrecy, or explanations that change during the conversation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important limits and expectations for the marketplace.

Does DirectOwner handle the transaction?

No. DirectOwner connects private buyers and sellers. The transaction takes place directly between the parties.

Does verification guarantee a listing is legitimate?

No. Review and moderation are intended to reduce risk, but buyers and sellers must still verify information, inspect vehicles, and complete their own due diligence.

Is DirectOwner a lender?

No. DirectOwner is not a lender. Any financing options, if available, are provided by independent third-party providers.

How do I report a suspicious listing or user?

Send the listing URL, screenshots, and a description of the concern to our support email. Do not send payment while the concern is unresolved.
