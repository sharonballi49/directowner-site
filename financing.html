Review before signing

Compare the total cost, interest rate, fees, and conditions before accepting any financing offer.

How financing fits into DirectOwner

  • DirectOwner may provide information or connect buyers with financing resources when available.
  • DirectOwner does not originate loans, approve applications, or set lender terms.
  • Not every vehicle, buyer, or transaction will qualify for financing.
  • Buyers should confirm the lender’s requirements before making commitments to a seller.

Before completing a purchase

  • Inspect the vehicle and consider an independent mechanic inspection.
  • Verify the seller’s identity, ownership, title, VIN, and any lien information.
  • Confirm how the lender will handle payment and title documentation.
  • Do not send money based only on urgency, screenshots, or unverified promises.
Important: DirectOwner is a marketplace, not a bank, credit union, lender, or loan broker. Financing may be offered by independent third parties where available. DirectOwner does not guarantee approval or financing availability.
