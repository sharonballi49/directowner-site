Frequently Asked Questions

How do I list my car or RV?

Visit the Sell page and submit your vehicle information through the listing form. Phase 1 listings are reviewed manually before publication.

Does DirectOwner handle payments or title transfer?

No. DirectOwner connects private buyers and sellers. Transactions and title transfer occur directly between the parties.

Are you a dealer or lender?

No. DirectOwner is not a dealership or lender. Financing, where available, is provided by independent third-party partners.

How do I report a suspicious listing?

Email support@getdirectowner.com with the listing link and a description of the concern.
