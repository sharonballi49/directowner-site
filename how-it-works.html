Trust & Safety Comes First

Use these practical steps to make informed decisions before completing a transaction.

Review Carefully

2

Verify Documents

Confirm the VIN, title, condition, ownership details, and any relevant vehicle history.

4

Report Concerns

Contact DirectOwner support if you notice suspicious activity or questionable listing information.

What Makes DirectOwner Different?

Traditional vehicle buying can involve dealership pressure, hidden markups, and middleman friction. DirectOwner focuses on owner-to-owner transparency so buyers can ask questions directly and sellers can retain more control.

Financing Guidance

Some buyers may need financing for a private-party purchase. DirectOwner may provide guidance or connections to third-party partners where available, but DirectOwner is not a lender and does not make loan decisions.

Important Reminder

DirectOwner is a marketplace platform. The final transaction takes place directly between buyer and seller. Both parties should complete their own due diligence, verify documents, and use safe meeting and payment practices.
