Privacy Policy

Learn how DirectOwner collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our marketplace.

Last updated: September 22, 2026
Early-stage marketplace: DirectOwner is continuing to develop its marketplace features. We aim to collect and use information needed to operate the site, manage listings, respond to support requests, and improve trust and safety.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details submitted through forms or support requests.
  • Vehicle or RV listing details, including make, model, year, mileage, location, price, descriptions, and photos.
  • Account information used to sign in and manage listings.
  • Messages, inquiries, or other communications sent to DirectOwner.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, and pages visited.
  • Usage information used to maintain and improve the website.

2. How We Use Information

DirectOwner may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts and vehicle listings.
  • Review, publish, and maintain marketplace listings.
  • Respond to support requests and inquiries.
  • Improve trust and safety, including investigating suspected fraud or abuse.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website.
  • Send important service-related communications.

3. How We Share Information

DirectOwner does not sell personal information. Information may be shared in limited circumstances:

  • When needed to provide a marketplace service requested by you.
  • With service providers that support hosting, authentication, storage, email, payments, or other website operations.
  • To investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or safety concerns.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

4. Listings and Public Information

Information included in an active marketplace listing may become publicly visible, including vehicle details, pricing, general location, descriptions, and images.

Do not include sensitive personal information, financial information, passwords, or documents containing personal identifiers in public listing descriptions or uploaded photos.

5. Cookies and Analytics

DirectOwner may use cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies to understand website performance, remember preferences, improve usability, and understand site traffic. Third-party services may collect information under their own privacy practices.

6. Data Security

DirectOwner takes reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. No website, email system, database, or online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained as long as reasonably necessary to operate the marketplace, manage accounts and listings, provide support, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

8. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for hosting, authentication, database and file storage, email, analytics, payment processing, financing-related referrals, or other operational needs. Those providers may have separate privacy policies and terms.

9. Children's Privacy

DirectOwner is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children. The marketplace is not intended for use by minors.

10. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact DirectOwner with questions about your information, account, or listings. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests, and some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the marketplace develops or our practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through our Contact page.

You may also email info@periwisehealth.com.
